# 2. The editor

The editor has four parts.

![The four parts of the editor](images/editor-01-overview.png)

1. **Toolbar**: undo, pages, the page layout, screen sizes, Preview, Export and help.
2. **Left panel**: three tabs.
   - **Add**: everything you can put on a page.
   - **Layers**: what's on the current page, as a list.
   - **Pages**: the pages of your website.
3. **Page**: your website, as visitors will see it. Click things to select them, drag them to move them.
4. **Right panel**: two tabs.
   - **Element**: the settings of whatever you selected on the page.
   - **Site**: settings for the whole website (title, colours, fonts, export).

When the editor is narrow, the left panel tabs show only their icons. Point at an icon to see its name.

**More room for the page.** Drag the edge of either side panel to make it wider or narrower, and double-click the edge to put it back. The two buttons at the far ends of the toolbar hide a panel completely, which gives the page the full window. Press them again to bring it back. The editor remembers your choice.

## The toolbar

![The toolbar](images/editor-02-toolbar.png)

1. **Undo** and **redo**.
2. **Page menu**: switch to another page of your website. The site title is shown next to it.
3. **Flow / Free**: how the page is laid out. *Flow* stacks sections under each other, like most websites; *Free* lets you place things anywhere on a screen of a fixed size. The button next to it shows a column guide (on a free page: a grid). See [Free layout](06-pages.md#free-layout-place-things-anywhere).
4. **Screen size**: see the page as it looks on a **desktop** computer, a **tablet** or a **phone**. See [Screen sizes and Preview](10-preview-and-screen-sizes.md).
5. **Edit / Preview**: *Edit* is where you build. *Preview* lets you use the page like a visitor would: open menus, filter projects, click buttons, follow links.
6. **Try it in your browser**: exports the website and opens it in your web browser, so videos, forms and your own scripts work too. See [Exporting](11-export.md).
7. **Export**: saves your website as real web pages you can put online. See [Exporting](11-export.md).
8. **Help (?)**: a quick tour of the editor, and this guide.
